# Shell / thicken sweep against OCCT

`tools/shell_sweep.mjs` generates seeded shell and thicken cases, runs them
through the kit wasm, and compares the volume with an OCP replay
(`tools/shell_sweep_ref.py`). Face removals are geometric (`top`, `side_ymin`);
the reference selects the same faces by position.

```sh
node tools/shell_sweep.mjs --samples 40 --seed 7 --only box_top --json out.json
node tools/shell_sweep.mjs --samples 40 --seed 7 --only box_top \
  --update-baseline tools/shell_sweep_baseline.json
```

Run one template at a time. A single process that walks every template can
stop responding after the first few hundred requests; the per-template runs
above are the reliable form. The baseline stores case ids and classes plus
the wasm hash, seed and sample count.

## Failure groups

| template | class | failing parameters | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| box_out | wrongVolume | all 40; rel 3.0e-2 .. 1.3e-1 | outward shell with the top removed keeps an extra roof slab; error scales with `w*d*t` |
| tube_top | shellFailed | 15/40; `ro` 14.8 .. 38.4, `ri` 4.4 .. 29.0, `h` 11.7 .. 50.9 | OCP thick solid succeeds |
| cone_top | wrongVolume | 38/40; rel 1.1e-1 .. 2.0e-1 | drafted-cylinder shell; base cone volume matches OCP |
| l_closed | wrongVolume | 19/40; rel 3.4e-2 .. 1.9e+1 | closed shell of a two-box L; the large errors leave most of the solid |
| l_closed | refused | 21/40 | thickness above the wall/feature limit |

The box/cylinder large-thickness refusals are correct behaviour. The four
groups above are volume-law or wall-construction gaps; see
`docs/mimo-brief-shell.md` for ranked repros.

## Passed

Single-face and two-face box shells (inward), cylinder top shell, shell after
a vertical-edge fillet, and thicken of planar, cylindrical and conical sheets
all passed 40/40. The cylindrical sheet is a loft between two equal circles
(`surfaceExtrude` of a circle currently returns no response, so it is not
used as the passing template).

## Parts thinner than the wall (`plate_thin`, `fin_thin`, `skirt_thin`)

Generated by their own seeded generator (from `--seed`), so the templates above keep their parameters and these keep
theirs whatever `--samples` is. `plate_thin`: a box no thicker than the wall (`t` = 1 to 1.5 times `h`), top removed.
`fin_thin`: a box with a fin on top (`ft` = 0.5 to 1.5 times the wall), bottom removed. `skirt_thin`: a cylinder with a
skirt on its top rim (`st` = 0.4 to 1.5 times the wall), bottom removed. The expected behaviour (MitCad 0.44) is that the
thin region stays solid, the rest hollows, and a warning names the faces left solid.

`node tools/shell_sweep.mjs --samples 10 --seed 7 --only plate_thin,fin_thin,skirt_thin` (branch wasm, 2026-11-05):

| template | n | ok | wrongVolume | refused | shellFailed | ref-fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plate_thin | 10 | 0 | 0 | 10 | 0 | 0 |
| fin_thin | 10 | 0 | 0 | 4 | 0 | 6 (kernel: refused) |
| skirt_thin | 10 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 10 | 0 |

* `plate_thin`: `shellTooThick`, the detail names both faces and the limit (8/10: "F2.C0 and F2.C1 are only 3.02 mm
  apart ... use at most 2.86 mm"; 2/10: "an edge reverses"). OCCT returns a volume for all 10, but it is the plate less a
  few mm3 (3181.8 for a 3186 mm3 plate), not a shell: no usable reference.
* `fin_thin`: `shellTooThick` "an edge reverses", with no face named, for every fin, also those thicker than the wall
  (`maxThickness` is about 0.45 times the fin). OCCT fails 6/10 and returns the unshelled solid volume for the other 4.
* `skirt_thin`: `shellFailed` "the cavity could not be cut" for all 10. OCCT returns volumes, but they match neither the
  solid nor "body hollowed, skirt solid" (e.g. 1632 against 27240 solid, 6754 expected): no usable reference.

Neither engine yet does what MitCad does here. The rows are in `tools/shell_sweep_baseline.json`; `--json` rows now
also carry `why` (the error params), `warn` (feature warnings) and `kernel` (the kernel's class where OCCT failed).
