Fillet oracle

Rolling-ball round / 45° chamfer of a flat rim, from the morphological opening (independent of any B-rep builder).

Outline: drag a point; click on the curve to add one; double-click (or shift-click) a point to remove it; alt-click toggles a corner (the curve breaks there, straight line between corners). Editing makes the outline a custom Catmull-Rom spline (kernel layer off). 3D: drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, shift-drag to pan.
3D (oracle surface, edge clipped to the exact outline)
